ToxiTech PocketGPU Phase 2 research add-on

Download and unpack into ~/ToxiTech-PocketGPU/ to add research notes and a blank measurement log. This add-on does not contain BIOS dumps and does not overwrite existing firmware files.

Example:
  unzip -n ~/Downloads/ToxiTech_PocketGPU_Phase2_Research.zip -d ~/ToxiTech-PocketGPU

The .fz source is only documented as a potential matching boardview; it has not been retrieved or decoded.
