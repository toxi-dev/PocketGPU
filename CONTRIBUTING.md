# Contributing to PocketGPU

Document measurements clearly: board marking/revision, photo orientation, meter mode, reference probe, measured value, and whether the board is completely unpowered.

Distinguish **MEASURED**, **INFERRED**, and **UNKNOWN**. A continuity beep is not a verified rail voltage or a fully established circuit connection.

Do not submit proprietary BIOS/VBIOS dumps, unauthorized paid boardview material, or third-party copyrighted repair manuals.

No design is safe to fabricate or energize until explicitly reviewed and validated.
